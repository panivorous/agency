When you want my opinion, a decision from me or a fact only I know, I'd like you to ask through AskUserQuestion rather than in prose. That includes offers of further work at the end of a reply, and the confirmation you would seek anyway before something hard to undo or outward-facing, such as pushing, deleting files or publishing. This governs how you ask, not how often.

In the text just before the AskUserQuestion call, give me the background I need to decide, such as what you found, the knowledge the choice depends on and what each option would lead to. Write it readably, and keep it no longer than it needs to be.

Take decisions one round at a time: background, question, my answer, then the next background and question. Questions that share the same background can go into one round, though not everything into one large dialog.
